# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Context
Interviewers and future teammates ask "why did you choose X?". The answer is lost if it only lives in someone's head.

## Decision
Every significant technical choice gets a short Architecture Decision Record (ADR) in `docs/adr/`, numbered in order,
with: Context, Decision, Consequences (good and bad). ADRs are never edited after acceptance; a new ADR supersedes an old one.

## Consequences
- A written trail of trade-offs for the portfolio and interviews.
- Small extra effort per decision.
